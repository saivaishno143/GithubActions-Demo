01 / About

More than deploying servers. I engineer the system behind them.

My focus is building infrastructure that is automated, reproducible, observable and easier for engineering teams to operate.

05 / Engineering Mindset

The principles behind my work.

01

Automate repetitive work.

If an operational task needs to happen repeatedly, it should eventually become a reliable and version-controlled process.

02

Infrastructure should be reproducible.

Environments should not depend on someone's memory or manual console clicks. Desired infrastructure belongs in code.

03

Understand before troubleshooting.

Good troubleshooting starts with understanding the system — processes, memory, networking, logs, dependencies and failure modes.
